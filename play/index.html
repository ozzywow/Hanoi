Tower of Hanoi Online – Free Speedrun Game

Play the classic Tower of Hanoi puzzle as a speedrun, right in your browser – no install and no sign-up. Move all the discs to the red pole as fast as you can, then see how your time would rank against the world rankings.

How to play Tower of Hanoi

  1. Only one disc may be moved at a time.
  2. No disc may be placed on top of a smaller disc.
  3. Move all the discs to the red pole.

Controls

Click or tap a pole to pick up its top disc, then click or tap another pole to drop it. You can also drag and drop. On a keyboard, press Space to start and J, K and L to select the left, middle and right pole.

Levels and world rankings

The web version has levels 3 and 4. Your web time is for practice and is compared with the live world rankings. For all levels, official world rankings, ghost races and replays, get the free app.
